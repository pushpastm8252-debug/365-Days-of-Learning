# 🎯 Interview Tips

- Know the difference between **print()** and **return**.
- Understand **Parameters vs Arguments**.
- Practice **Recursion** and **Lambda Functions**.
- Learn **Local vs Global Variables**.
- Understand **Default, Positional, Keyword, *args, and **kwargs Arguments**.
- Practice writing functions without looking at notes.
- Be able to explain every program step-by-step.
- Focus on logic building instead of memorizing code.
